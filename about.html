Who We Are

West Bengal Society of Pediatric Rheumatology

The West Bengal Society of Pediatric Rheumatology (WBSPR) is a professional medical society bringing together pediatricians, pediatric rheumatologists, and researchers committed to improving the diagnosis, treatment, and long-term care of children with rheumatic and autoimmune conditions — including Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis, Systemic Lupus Erythematosus, Vasculitis, and Kawasaki Disease.

Our members range from senior faculty to enthusiastic post-graduate trainees, all united by a common goal: ensuring that every child with a rheumatic condition in West Bengal receives timely, specialist, and compassionate care.

Our Mission

To improve the health, growth, and quality of life of children affected by rheumatic and autoimmune diseases across West Bengal, through clinical excellence, education, and collaborative research.

Our Vision

To be the leading academic voice for pediatric rheumatology in West Bengal — advancing early diagnosis, evidence-based treatment, and awareness so that every child with a rheumatic condition can thrive.

West Bengal Society of Pediatric Rheumatology

Better care for children living with rheumatic disease

A community of pediatricians and rheumatologists working together on early recognition, sound treatment and lifelong learning across West Bengal.

What We Do

Advancing pediatric rheumatology, together

01

Academic Programs

Regular CME sessions, case discussions and workshops on Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis, Lupus, Vasculitis and other pediatric rheumatic conditions.

02

Awareness and Outreach

Helping pediatricians and families recognise childhood rheumatic diseases early, so every child reaches specialist care on time.

03

Training and Mentorship

Training modules, mentorship and a platform for paper and poster presentations for trainees in pediatric rheumatology.

Our Mission

Timely diagnosis changes a child's future

WBSPR brings the state's pediatric and rheumatology community together to share knowledge, support colleagues and raise standards of care for children with autoimmune and inflammatory conditions.

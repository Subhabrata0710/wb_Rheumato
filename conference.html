About The Event

The Society's Flagship Scientific Event

The WBSPR Annual Conference 2026 is the flagship academic event of the West Bengal Society of Pediatric Rheumatology, bringing together pediatric rheumatologists, pediatricians, and trainees from across the state.

The conference will feature scientific sessions, case discussions, and updates on the diagnosis and management of pediatric rheumatic and autoimmune diseases, along with opportunities for trainees to present their work.

  • Scientific sessions and guest orations
  • Interactive case discussions and panels
  • Paper and poster presentations for trainees
  • Networking with peers and senior faculty
